# Matthew Rojas

## Recruiting leader building practical AI-powered hiring systems

I am a Talent Acquisition and recruiting operations leader with 15+ years of experience supporting technical organizations across infrastructure, cloud, software, data centers, and semiconductor talent.

At AWS, I helped scale a centralized recruiting organization from **3 to approximately 40 recruiters in seven months** and reduced recruiter onboarding time from **eight weeks to four**. My work combines technical recruiting, operational design, stakeholder partnership, team development, analytics, and responsible AI adoption.

### What I build

- AI-assisted recruiting workflows
- Hiring-manager intake and calibration tools
- Technical sourcing and outreach systems
- Recruiting analytics and capacity models
- Recruiter onboarding and operating playbooks
- Responsible-AI practices for Talent Acquisition
- Practical AI tools that help job seekers organize their search, prepare for interviews, and communicate their experience clearly
